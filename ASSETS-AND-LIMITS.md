# Assets and limitations

**Real:** the 20 client photos (c01–c20). c16–c20 show one kitchen in five finishes; they are used as finish examples,
not as separate 3D models. They look digitally produced, so treat them as illustrative until confirmed.

**Prototype (not real models):** all 3D kitchens, the TV unit and the wardrobe are generated in code; wood and stone
textures are generated at runtime. They are not measured copies of any installation.

**Still needed for production**
* Professionally modelled `.glb` kitchens / TV unit / wardrobe (or measured drawings), and photographed texture sets.
* A form service URL (`formEndpoint`) or a business email if enquiries should arrive without WhatsApp/SMS/call.
* Photographs of wardrobes, beds, dining/dressing tables and bathroom units (services 05–11 have no photo yet).
* Dressing table and bathroom storage 3D models (not built).
* Confirmation that the supplied number is on WhatsApp.
* Absolute `og:image` URL in `index.html` once the final domain is known.

**Not tested:** real-GPU performance, physical touch gestures, Safari/Firefox, tablet widths, screen readers, the
earlier lightbox/gallery interactions after the photo swap beyond load checks.
