# Assets, reference and limits

**Reference site:** the link supplied opens D'LIFE Interiors (dlifeinteriors.com). I could read its page structure and
content but could not render how it looks, so its exact colours, fonts and imagery were not matched. The redesign follows its
layout language (utility bar, white header, hero + facts strip, service tiles, uppercase titles, big photography, dark
bands) in this business's own palette. None of its text, offers, prices, warranties, numbers or branding was used.
Send a screenshot or colour/font preferences if you want it closer.

**Photos still missing (none exist in the supplied files):** sliding wardrobes, beds and bedroom interiors, dining tables,
dressing tables, bathroom mirror boxes and storage units. Those services appear as text only. The wardrobe photos supplied so far show hinged doors, not sliding doors.

**Also note:** c16–c20 (one kitchen in five colours) look digitally produced; they are shown as finish examples only.

**Not tested:** real-GPU performance, physical touch hardware beyond emulation, Safari/Firefox, tablet widths, screen readers,
and the live Vercel deployment (only local production builds and a sub-folder static host were tested).
